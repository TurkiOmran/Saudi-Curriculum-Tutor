## 4 Chant Along

### I Never Found Gold Anywhere Until I Got Back Home

I've been to Jamaica.
I've been to Japan.
I've traveled all over the world.
I've sailed on a ship and flown in a plane.
But I've never found diamonds or gold.

I studied in Paris when I was a teen—
I looked for happiness there.
Then I went to London where I saw the queen.
Worked at a job in Mayfair.
But I've never found gold anywhere.
No, I never found gold anywhere.

I've been a sailor, a waiter, a writer.
I've been a teller, a driver, a fighter.

When I finished school, I worked in a bank.
Then I fought in a terrible war.
I shot with a rifle and rode in a tank.
But I've never broken the law.
But I've never found gold anywhere.
No, I never found gold anywhere.

I've been a sailor, a waiter, a writer.
I've been a teller, a driver, a fighter.
