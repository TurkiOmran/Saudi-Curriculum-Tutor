## Writing Corner

1. A witness report describes the events of an incident in the order they happened. It answers the five W's (who, what, where, when, why) and explains how the incident happened.
2. Use connecting words such as: and, but, because, so, and when. I was in the park when the accident happened. There was ice on the road, so the driver lost control and hit a tree.

B. Read the witness report. Does it answer who, what, where, when, why, and how?
