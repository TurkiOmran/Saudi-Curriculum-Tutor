6 Conversation

Ali: So, ... What's new?
Badr: I bought myself a computer.
Ali: What kind?
Badr: A laptop. Laptops are friendlier to use and are cheaper than other computers.
Ali: Does it have Internet?
Badr: Not yet. I'm going to install that next week.
Ali: I don't like laptops. I prefer desktops. Desktops are easier to use, especially if you have to write and print a lot. Laptops... you have to recharge them all the time, and their screen is much smaller.
Badr: I don't agree. Laptops are the best. You can surf the Net, send emails, and study anywhere. You don't have to wait until you get home.
Ali: Well, I still think desktops are much more convenient than laptops.
Badr: Yeah, but you can't take one with you.
