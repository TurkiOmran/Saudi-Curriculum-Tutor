## A Complete the conversation. Use my, mine, your, yours, and ours.

Mom: Whose is this? Is it (1) _______________, Sahar?

Mom: Is this yours, Amal?

Amal: No, it's not (3) _______________ vest.

Mom: Look, Fahd. Is this (5) _______________?

Fahd: No, it's not (6) _______________.

Amal: Isn't (7) _______________ vest red, Fahd?

Fahd: Yes, it is.

Amal, Fahd, and Sahar: It's not (8) _______________.

Fahd: Wait, Mom. That is (9) _______________ vest.
