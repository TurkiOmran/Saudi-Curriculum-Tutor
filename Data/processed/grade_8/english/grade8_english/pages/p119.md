## Travel the World Over

![img-306.jpeg](img-306.jpeg)

![img-307.jpeg](img-307.jpeg)

I'm going to travel all around Europe, Ski the Alps at great heights.
I'm going to climb the Eiffel Tower, And, in Madrid, watch the bull fights.
I'm going to sail to the Greek islands, And taste Italian cuisine,
Stroll through markets in London, And see the palace of the king.

I'm going to explore all over Asia, Trek all along the Great Wall, Ride a rickshaw in Shanghai, Hike the Himalayas in Nepal.
I'm going to ride elephants in India, Drive a 4x4 in Arabian sand, I'm going to smell the cherry blossoms, And take the bullet train in Japan.

![img-308.jpeg](img-308.jpeg)

![img-309.jpeg](img-309.jpeg)

I'm going to North and South America, Up high to Machu Picchu in Peru, Catch a cab in New York City, And cross the Great Lakes by canoe.
I'm going to snowmobile in Alaska.
I'm going to raft down the Amazon, Listen to the roar of Venezuelan Falls, And cruise right around Cape Horn.

I'll go on wildlife safaris in Africa, Dive in Australia's Great Barrier Reef.
I'm going to travel the world over, And see things beyond belief.
