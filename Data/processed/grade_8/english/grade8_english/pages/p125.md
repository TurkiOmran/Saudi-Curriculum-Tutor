# 6 Conversation 7

Tariq: So, I hear you're going to move to Abha.
Adel: Yeah. I got a great job there.
Tariq: What work will you do?
Adel: I'm going to be a trainee in an international hotel.
Tariq: When do you plan to move?
Adel: I'll probably go next month. I want to spend the rest of the summer with my family before I move away.
Tariq: How long will you stay?
Adel: It depends.
Tariq: Depends on what?
