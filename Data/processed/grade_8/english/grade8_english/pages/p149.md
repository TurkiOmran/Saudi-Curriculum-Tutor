# 6 Conversation

Michael: What's the most awesome experience you've ever had?

Andrew: Definitely when I went shark diving. Have you ever heard of it?

Michael: No, never.

Andrew: Well, I went shark diving in Gansbaai, South Africa. It's one of the best places in the world to see the great white sharks up close.

Michael: You're out of your mind! You'll never catch me diving in the middle of sharks.

Andrew: It's not like that. You go out on a boat to a place called "Shark Alley," and you go down inside a cage. The people on the boat throw out big pieces of fish tied to a rope in order to attract the sharks. The sharks come up real close, and frequently they knock the cage with their heads.

Michael: Weren't you afraid?

Andrew: To be honest, I was scared to death.

![img-399.jpeg](img-399.jpeg)

FYI "Gansbaai" is the Afrikaans word that means "goose bay."

# Real Talk

Definitely = expressing a high degree of certainty
You're out of your mind! = You're crazy!
To be honest = to tell the truth
scared to death = very frightened

# About the Conversation

1. Where did Andrew go on his vacation?
2. What kind of experience was it?
3. How do they attract the sharks?
4. Was he scared?
5. What does Michael think?
