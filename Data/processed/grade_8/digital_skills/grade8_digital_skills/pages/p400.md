# INPUT AND OUTPUT DEVICES

![img-674.jpeg](img-674.jpeg)

## INPUT DEVICES

An input device is a part of the computer that lets you enter data, such as a keyboard or a mouse.

## OUTPUT DEVICES

An output device shows the results from the computer, such as a monitor or a printer.
