## تدريب 1

اكتب ناتج المقطع البرمجي التالي:

```python
import openpyxl
wb = openpyxl.load_workbook(filename="nutrition.xlsx")
sheet_obj = wb["arabic"]
rows = sheet_obj.iter_rows(min_row=3, max_row=5, min_col=1, max_col=11)
for row in rows:
for cell in row:
print(cell.value, end=" ")
print("")
```
